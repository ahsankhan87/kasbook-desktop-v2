📊 THE FIX EXPLAINED:

BEFORE (Broken SQL):
  LEFT JOIN acc_bank_reconciliation_items I
	ON I.entry_id = E.id
	AND I.reconciliation_id = @LatestRecId  ← When NULL, entire join is NULL!
  WHERE E.account_id = @BankAccountId       ← Filter doesn't work correctly

AFTER (Fixed SQL):
  LEFT JOIN acc_bank_reconciliation_items I
	ON I.entry_id = E.id
	AND I.reconciliation_id = @LatestRecId
	AND @LatestRecId IS NOT NULL             ← Only join when @LatestRecId exists
  WHERE E.account_id = @BankAccountId       ← Filter works correctly now!

WHY IT WORKS:
  - When @LatestRecId is NULL, the LEFT JOIN is skipped
  - The WHERE clause E.account_id = @BankAccountId properly filters entries
  - Only entries for the selected bank account are returned ✅
